This project is designed to show how organizations can:
- align technical controls with business risk
- track evidence for compliance and internal audits
- present cyber risk in a way that leadership can understand
- connect operating risk to governance and accountability

I’m using this work to move from a compliance and governance background into a stronger cyber-risk and security governance role.
