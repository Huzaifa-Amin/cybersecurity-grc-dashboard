Case study

Cybersecurity GRC Dashboard

A governance, risk, and compliance dashboard built to show how control ownership, evidence, and framework mapping can be presented in a clear, executive-facing way.

The project focuses on a realistic challenge in cyber risk programs: translating technical controls into understandable business language without losing governance rigor.

CONFIRMED: EU/US demand is strong for GRC and risk governance roles

Project goal

Demonstrate control maturity, ownership, and remediation prioritization for GRC work.

A small-organization GRC starter, not a certified compliance service or production SOC platform.

Architecture

Flow

Role-aware Streamlit app → validation and risk engine → SQLAlchemy → shared PostgreSQL or local SQLite → dashboard and exports

What it proves

Control ownership, evidence tracking, governance visibility, and risk prioritization.

Threat model

Threats considered include stale evidence, weak ownership, misaligned control definitions, and incomplete risk prioritization.

Mitigations include structured ownership records, framework mapping, and clear remediation actions.

Residual risk: One app/database deployment with application-enforced organization isolation; no SSO/MFA, high availability, evidence file storage, automatic asset exposure matching, or load-tested capacity. Framework mappings and records require organizational review.
